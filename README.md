Fluxion Filipino Restaurant 🇵🇭

A self-initiated Filipino restaurant website concept created to explore modern UI/UX while presenting Filipino food through a clean and engaging digital experience.

📌 Project Overview

Fluxion Filipino Restaurant is a web design and development project focused on creating a modern digital experience for a Filipino restaurant.

The project was designed around the idea of combining Filipino food culture with a contemporary web interface, giving customers an easy way to discover the restaurant, explore its offerings, and learn more about the brand.

🎯 The Problem

A restaurant website needs to do more than simply display information. It should quickly communicate:

What the restaurant offers
What makes the brand unique
What customers can expect
Where to find important information
How to navigate the menu and restaurant experience

The challenge was to present Filipino cuisine in a way that felt modern, accessible, and visually engaging without losing its cultural identity.

💡 The Solution

I designed and developed a responsive restaurant website with a strong focus on visual storytelling, food presentation, and user experience.

The interface uses structured content and product-focused sections to guide visitors through the restaurant's digital experience.

✨ Key Features
🍽️ Filipino Food Showcase

Food offerings are presented through a visual layout designed to make the dishes easy to discover.

🇵🇭 Filipino-inspired Branding

The visual direction combines contemporary web design with elements inspired by Filipino food and culture.

📖 Restaurant Information

Important restaurant details are organized into clear sections for easy access.

📱 Responsive Design

The website adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

🎨 Custom UI/UX

The interface was designed from the ground up rather than relying on a generic restaurant template.
